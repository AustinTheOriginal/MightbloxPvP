📖 How to Play

Movement

  • W A S D — move
  • Space — jump
  • Shift — crouch & raise shield
  • Mouse — look (click to lock for free spinning)

Combat

  • Left click — attack / mine (bow: hold to charge, release to fire)
  • Right click — eat steak; place TNT/block; place block
  • 1–6, scroll, or tap a slot — switch item
  • Mobile: tap right side to attack/place; hold to mine or eat

Multiplayer

  • Share your Room Code with friends — everyone in the same room can fight each other
  • Other players appear with their name above their head
  • PvP works the same as vs AI: swing to hit, use kits strategically

Health

  • 10 hearts (20 HP) + hunger bar
  • Eat steak to refill hunger; full hunger regens health

Kits

  • Fighter: sword 4 dmg (8 crit), axe 6 dmg (7 crit, very slow, disables shields 5s)
  • Demolitionist: flame bow up to 4 dmg + TNT carts (max 10 explosion damage)
